About Us

At TripMate, We believe every journey is an opportunity to discover something new. Founded in 2019 by Ethan Carter and Ryan Mitchell, JTravels began with a shared passion for discovering new places and making travel easier for everyone. Based in California, USA, Ethan and Ryan often found themselves spending hours searching different websites to compare destinations, accommodations, activities, and travel options. They realized that planning a vacation could be much more complicated than the actual journey.

Our Origin

In 2020, they decided to turn their idea into reality and started building a simple travel platform that would bring useful travel information together in one place. Over the years, their small idea grew into JTravels, a platform created to inspire travelers to discover new destinations, plan memorable vacations, and experience the world with confidence.Today, JTravels continues to follow the vision that started it all — Travel Beyond Boundaries. 🌎✈️
